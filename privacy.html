Privacy policy

How EdiTech Solutions handles information you choose to share through this website.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Information we collect

When you use our enquiry form, we ask for your name, email address, selected service, and message. A phone number is optional. Our hosting and database services may also process technical information required for normal website operation and security.

Why we use it

We use enquiry details to understand requests, respond to potential clients and manage our communications. We do not sell submitted contact details.

Storage and access

Messages submitted to the website are stored in our configured Supabase database and can be accessed by authorised EdiTech administrators. We use database access rules to restrict who can read these messages.

Retention and deletion

We keep enquiries only as long as reasonably needed for communication and business records, subject to applicable obligations. You may ask for access, correction or deletion by using the official contact details once published.

Third-party services

The website uses Bootstrap, Google Fonts and Supabase. These services may process technical data under their own policies.

Changes

We may update this notice when the website or our processes change. The latest version will be published on this page.
